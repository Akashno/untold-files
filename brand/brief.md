# Random Talker: what it is

A website (randomtalker.com) that connects you with a random stranger for a one-to-one **voice** call.

True facts you may use:
- Tap the call button and you're matched with a random person in seconds.
- Voice only. No video, no camera, nobody sees your face or your room.
- No signup needed to start, no download: it works in any browser, including on your phone. It only asks for your microphone.
- Everyone gets a random nickname like "Dizzy Wolf", "Messy Toast", "Quiet Clown", "Biggie Pizza", and a cartoon avatar.
- Skip anyone in one tap; "auto-call after skip" connects you to the next person straight away.
- Report someone in seconds from the call screen. Images in chat are blurred until you tap them.
- Text chat during the call, if you'd rather type. Messages in another language (e.g. Hindi) can be translated with a tap.
- Interests: add a tag like #english, #music, #gaming, #movies, #travel, #food, #books and get matched with someone who shares it.
- 7 games you play together inside the call: UNO, Ludo, Tic-Tac-Toe, Chain Reaction, Battleship, Sudoku, Tetris.
- Add someone as a friend after a good call.
- Real people only ("No AI. Just humans.").
- Free to start. A Premium plan exists (priority matching, ad-free), so say "free" or "free to start", never "100% free" or "completely free".
- Popular for practising English (and Hindi, Bengali, Filipino, Bahasa and more) with real people, and for late-night conversations. Big in India, open worldwide.

Never claim or imply: video calls, dating or hookups, user numbers or statistics, awards, guarantees, that it's "safe" in absolute terms, that calls are recorded or not recorded, anything about AI, prices, features not listed above, or that it's affiliated with Omegle. Don't mention competitors by name except Omegle as nostalgia (it shut down in 2023).

# Voice and tone

Warm, playful, relatable, a bit cheeky; like a friend telling you about an app they like. Gen Z / young millennial audience (18-28), many in India, many learning English. Simple words, short sentences. Never salesy, never cringe, no hard-sell. Humour beats hype. No emojis in on-screen text.

# Nicknames and avatars

Strangers in match, call and chat scenes get an app-style random nickname: Adjective + Noun,
playful, at most 16 characters. Invent a new one every time; never reuse "Dizzy Wolf",
"Messy Toast", "Quiet Clown", "Biggie Pizza" or any name listed under "names already used".
Vary the avatars between videos.
